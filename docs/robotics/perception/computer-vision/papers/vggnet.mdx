# VGGNet

VGGNet is a deep convolutional neural network architecture 
developed by the Visual Geometry Group (VGG) at the University of Oxford.
It was introduced in 2014 and is known for its simplicity and effectiveness
in image classification tasks. Team members include Karen Simonyan and
