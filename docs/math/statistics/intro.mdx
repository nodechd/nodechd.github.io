# Introduction

This section focuses on statistics

We divide statistics into two main categories: 
descriptive statistics and inferential statistics.

Descriptive statistics involves summarizing and describing the main features of a dataset, 
while inferential statistics involves making predictions or inferences about a population 
based on a sample of data.
